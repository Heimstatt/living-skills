# Living Checklist — Advocatus Diaboli

*Template example. The entries below are illustrative — written to show the format, not real observations. Replace them with your own.*

**Purpose:** Application experience layer — review patterns collected while applying the skill.
Grows with every session. Never merged back into SKILL.md.

---

## Entry Format

```
### [YYYY-MM-DD] — [Analysis type / domain] · *unconfirmed (1 observation)*
**Learning:** What pattern was found / what failed
**Why it matters:** Structural reason it was missed
**Rule:** Concrete check to apply in future sessions
**Observed by:** <instance> (YYYY-MM-DD)
```

When a second instance hits the same pattern, it removes the marker and appends itself
instead of opening a new entry:
`**Observed by:** instance-a (YYYY-MM-DD), confirmed by instance-b (YYYY-MM-DD)`

## Entries

### YYYY-MM-DD — Counterargument weakened before it was answered · *illustrative*
**Learning:** The review listed a counterargument in its weakest form, rebutted that version and marked the category as resolved.
**Why it matters:** A rebuttal of a strawman feels like progress and lowers the weakness count without testing the analysis.
**Rule:** State each counterargument in the strongest form its proponents would accept before evaluating it. If you cannot, mark it as not yet examined.
**Observed by:** <instance> (YYYY-MM-DD)

---

### YYYY-MM-DD — Stop criterion reached by relabelling · *illustrative*
**Learning:** In the last iteration, open weaknesses were reclassified as "acceptable gaps" so the stop thresholds were met, but the analysis itself had not changed.
**Why it matters:** The iteration limit creates pressure to finish, and the distinction between weakness and acceptable gap is a judgement call.
**Rule:** Every weakness moved to "acceptable gap" needs a stated reason and must appear under "Remaining gaps" in the revision log.
**Observed by:** <instance> (YYYY-MM-DD)
