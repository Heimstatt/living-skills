# Design Market Creation Strategy

Reconstruct a market's current logic into a falsifiable alternative value configuration. Use Blue
Ocean Strategy as a named generative lens, preserve source evidence and assumptions, and hand back
a Market Creation Hypothesis rather than declaring an uncontested market proven.

## Required inputs

- a defined current market, category, or bounded search space;
- known alternatives, including nonconsumption where relevant;
- observed or clearly hypothetical customer/noncustomer evidence;
- current competing factors, cost drivers, and constraints to the extent known;
- the decision the calling line unit retains.

If no market or search-space boundary exists, return to opportunity framing or discovery. Do not
fill the Blue Ocean method with invented category assumptions.

## Workflow

### 1. Reconstruct the current logic

Describe who currently competes for which buyers, what factors receive investment, what tradeoffs
are treated as inevitable, and which actors or uses are excluded. Mark unsupported statements as
hypotheses.

### 2. Inspect alternative demand and utility

Look across alternatives, noncustomers, the buyer experience, and practical barriers to utility.
Do not treat dissatisfaction or nonconsumption as proof of willingness to switch or pay.

### 3. Generate four-action decisions

For the current competing factors, propose what to:

- eliminate because it adds cost without sufficient buyer value;
- reduce below prevailing assumptions;
- raise above prevailing assumptions;
- create where the current market offers little or nothing.

Give every action an evidence basis or explicit hypothesis and a plausible cost consequence.

### 4. Describe the alternative value profile

State the intended divergence from current alternatives in plain language. Check for focus,
meaningful divergence, internal coherence, and a concise value logic. A long feature list is not a
new value profile.

### 5. Test strategic sequence coherence

Examine in order:

1. **Buyer utility:** is there a compelling improvement for a defined person and circumstance?
2. **Accessible price:** could target buyers reasonably adopt at the hypothesized price?
3. **Target cost:** could the model plausibly deliver at that price and acceptable economics?
4. **Adoption:** what stakeholder, trust, capability, legal, or behavioral barriers could block it?

This is a hypothesis audit, not proof. Record the decisive evidence gap at each stage.

### 6. Return a Market Creation Hypothesis

Use the asset template. Include the current logic, proposed reconstruction, evidence state,
strategic-sequence gaps, fatal counter-hypothesis, and smallest next test. Return it through
`strategy` to the accountable line unit when called by the staff lead.

## Boundaries

- Do not reproduce proprietary canvases or workbook pages; use the original textual artifact here.
- Do not certify a “blue ocean,” market size, demand, willingness to pay, or unit economics.
- Do not replace positioning, offer design, product discovery, or customer research.
- Do not use the method for unbounded idea generation without a current logic to reconstruct.
- Do not force differentiation and lower cost claims when their mechanism is not explicit.

## Stop criterion

Stop when a coherent alternative value profile exists, every eliminate/reduce/raise/create decision
has a rationale, utility-price-cost-adoption gaps are visible, one fatal counter-hypothesis is named,
and the smallest next evidence action is assigned back to the accountable line unit.

## After action

Add only repeatable application failures to `living-checklist.md`. Keep the project's hypothesis
and test results outside the skill. Update `revisionslog.md` only when the method changes.
