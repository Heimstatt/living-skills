**Purpose:** Experience layer for applying distinctiveness challenges.

## Entries

### YYYY-MM-DD — A head start counted as defensibility · *illustrative*
**Scope:** Zero to One | Durability
**Insight:** Being first in a niche can be presented as a moat although nothing prevents a
better-resourced alternative from copying the offer once demand is visible.
**Why it matters:** Timing advantages look strong at the moment of review and decay exactly when
the concept succeeds.
**Rule:** For every defensibility claim, name the mechanism that still holds after a competitor
has copied the visible offer, and the observation that would show it failing.

### YYYY-MM-DD — A contrarian thesis without a falsifier · *illustrative*
**Scope:** Zero to One | Independent thesis
**Insight:** A concept was framed as "what others have not understood yet", but the review could
not say which observation would show the thesis to be wrong.
**Why it matters:** An unfalsifiable contrarian claim reads as insight and protects the concept
from every counterargument.
**Rule:** Record the independent thesis together with one observable falsifier. If none can be
named, label the thesis a belief, not a distinctiveness finding.
