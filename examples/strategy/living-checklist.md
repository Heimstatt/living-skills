# Living Checklist — Strategy Staff Function

*Template example. The entries below are illustrative — written to show the format, not real observations. Replace them with your own.*

**Purpose:** Experience layer for routing and integrating cross-functional strategy work.

## Management

**Entry threshold:** Add only when the same strategy-routing failure would likely recur.
**Boundary:** Project advice stays with the project; role methods stay in child skills.

## Entries

### YYYY-MM-DD — Advisory returned without a retained decision · *illustrative*
**Scope:** Integration | Return path
**Insight:** A specialist result was delivered as a general recommendation, but the routing step
had never named which decision the line unit still had to take.
**Why it matters:** Without a named decision, the advisory either gets ignored or quietly becomes
the decision, and Strategy takes over ownership it should not have.
**Rule:** Do not route until the accountable line unit and its retained decision are written down;
in the integrated advisory, state how the finding changes or does not change that decision.

### YYYY-MM-DD — Two lenses blended into one verdict · *illustrative*
**Scope:** Routing | Multiple specialists
**Insight:** A question was sent to two specialist roles at once and their results were merged
into a single recommendation, so neither lens nor its limitations stayed visible.
**Why it matters:** A blended verdict looks more robust than either input while hiding where the
lenses disagree.
**Rule:** Route to one specialist by default. If two are needed, run them as an explicit sequence
and report each finding with its named lens before integrating.
