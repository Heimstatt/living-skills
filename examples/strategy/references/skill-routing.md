# Strategy Staffing and Routing Registry

Read this registry before delegating strategic work.

| State signal | Required role / route | Required input | Expected artifact | Staffing status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current market rules, competing factors, utility, or noncustomers may need reconstruction | Market Creation & Value Innovation / `design-market-creation-strategy` | defined current market or search space, alternatives, available customer/noncustomer evidence, constraints | Market Creation Hypothesis | Staffed |
| A concept's uniqueness, independent-thinking claim, defensibility, distribution, or durability needs challenge | Strategic Distinctiveness / `assess-strategic-distinctiveness` | concept and claimed delta, current evidence, alternatives/competition, distribution assumptions | Strategic Distinctiveness Review | Staffed |
| Competitive positioning for a chosen segment must be designed | Marketing Positioning / `marketing` | supported opportunity, segment, alternatives, evidence | positioning route or capability gap | Vacant |
| Portfolio, business-unit, or resource-allocation strategy is required | Corporate/Portfolio Strategy / `design-portfolio-strategy` | portfolio, objectives, constraints, economics | portfolio choice record | Vacant |

## Routing rules

1. Choose market creation when the job is to reconstruct market boundaries or the value profile.
2. Choose distinctiveness when a stated concept needs an independent challenge about uniqueness,
   defensibility, distribution, or durability.
3. Market creation may produce a concept that distinctiveness later challenges. That is an explicit
   pipeline, not a blended single method.
4. Do not route ordinary marketing positioning to Market Creation merely because competitors are
   mentioned.
5. Strategy returns advice to the accountable line unit; routing does not transfer ownership.
